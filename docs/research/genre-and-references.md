# Genre, sound recipes and the reference tracks

Research pass of 2026-10-17. Page fetching was blocked, so web claims come from search summaries.
*(unverified)* marks domain knowledge or a single weak source. The KiloMix section is hard data,
decoded from the original .rbs song file.

## 1. Gear by genre

**1996-99 trance and prog house**
- **Paul van Dyk, "For an Angel":** Juno-60, Juno-106, JX-1, TR-808; 138 BPM, G major
  ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/features/how-paul-van-dyks-angel-changed-trance-forever),
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_an_Angel),
  [Tunebat](https://tunebat.com/Info/For-an-Angel-Paul-van-Dyk/3PbDqZ1VMsBTjXCbs6Jv7P)).
- **Energy 52, "Café del Mar":** Super JV, Akai S3000, Nord Lead, Juno-6, E-mu Orbit
  ([Sonicstate](https://www.sonicstate.com/news/2022/08/03/the-synths-of-energy-52s-cafe-del-mar)).
  The Nord Lead (1995) and Orbit (1996) postdate the 1993 original, so this probably describes remixes.
- **System F, "Out of the Blue":** an early Dutch JP-8000 supersaw and a TR-909
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Out_of_the_Blue_(System_F_song))). Sandstorm was made on a
  JP-8080 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_JP-8000)).
- **Sasha:** Atari with Notator, an Oberheim Xpander, Kyma via BT
  ([Gearnews](https://gearnews.com/rave-superstar-how-to-sound-like-sasha), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xpander_(EP))).
- **Way Out West (Wisternoff):** Jupiter-8 "for short stabs… really sharp envelopes"; a Soundcraft
  Ghost desk as an instrument ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/tech/in-pictures-jody-wisternoff-555080)).
- **BT:** Juno-106 first, plus Jupiter-8 and Nord Lead ("frizzy, chilly")
  ([MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/me-in-my-studio-bt-roland-made-an-actual-unicorn-here)).
- **Novation Supernova (1998):** shaped prog house and breaks 1999-2005
  ([t90s](https://t90s.substack.com/p/10-tracks-novation-supernova)).
- **Era-defining synths:** Nord Lead / Nord Lead 2 (1997), Access Virus A (1997), JP-8000/8080,
  Supernova, JV-1080/2080, Korg Trinity and M1, Waldorf Pulse and Microwave, Yamaha CS1x and AN1x,
  SH-101, TB-303 (acid trance). Effects: Lexicon PCM, Yamaha SPX, Alesis Quadraverb and Midiverb,
  Roland SDE, Eventide H3000, Alesis 3630, Drawmer gates, Mackie desks *(unverified)*.
- **Studio habits:** 3-4 shared FX sends with EQ the only per-channel processing; onboard synth FX
  ("everything shares a phaser"); desk saturation; layered kicks from samplers drifting slightly
  ([KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8555143), [KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8504914)).

**Goa**
- "Almost all goa tracks have a 303… a lot have two"; bass vs acid is whether the resonance is up.
  The SH-101 was called the most-used synth, alongside Juno-6/106; JD-800 "all over
  Etnica/Pleiadians/Crop Circle" (Trip Tonite opens on its factory "Killer Pad"); also MC-202, Nord
  Lead, K2000, the Virus from about 1998. Phrygian is "the most common mode"; tempo sweet spot 135-145
  ([Gearspace goa thread](https://gearspace.com/board/electronic-music-instruments-and-electronic-music-production/846372-ultimate-goa-trance-technique-thread.html),
  [90s psy thread](https://gearspace.com/board/electronic-music-instruments-and-electronic-music-production/1051195-90s-psy-goa-trance.html)).
- Simon Posford (Hallucinogen): a Mackie 32:8 on everything, a real MS-20 for its filter
  ([SOS](https://www.soundonsound.com/people/younger-brother)). The "Twisted"-era rig is a forum
  user's reconstruction *(unverified)*: Atari, Akai, K2000, SH-101, OSCar, Ensoniq DP/4, Quadraverb
  ([ModWiggler](https://www.modwiggler.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=224384)).
- Juno Reactor: Ben Watkins credits a Raindirk Symphony desk
  ([MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/juno-reactor-bible-of-dreams)).

**2020s throwback (two scenes)**
- Dreamy neo-trance, about 128-155 BPM: Oprofessionell (Filip Storsveen) co-runs Oslo's Ute.Rec, drawing
  on 90s goa and ambient ([RA](https://ra.co/promoters/103729),
  [Minimal Collective](https://www.minimalcollective.digital/editorial/ute-rec)). Bliss Inc. (St
  Petersburg, Radiant Love): about 130 BPM, a "1994 palette", drum breaks
  ([Rubadub](https://rubadub.co.uk/products/hacking-the-planet-ep)). Kalahari Oyster Cult: "90s tropes…
  with modern sound design" ([Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1508142-label-of-the-month-kalahari-oyster-cult)).
- Fast hard/euro-trance revival: DJ Heartstring (Berlin, from 2021, FL Studio and Maschine)
  ([Mixmag](https://mixmag.net/feature/dj-heartstring-interview-cover-feature-berlin-trance-techno-dance-music)).
- Kettama is tangential (house, speed garage, rave); Sweely is French house, not trance.
- These artists mostly work in a DAW.

**Prog breaks**
- 125-140 BPM ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu_skool_breaks)); Hybrid's "Finished
  Symphony" about 132. Framewerk only debuted in 2012 (previously Manhattan)
  ([RA](https://de.ra.co/dj/framewerk/biography)), so "Framewerk-style" is the modern continuation of
  the 1999-2005 Hybrid/Burufunk sound. Typical gear *(unverified)*: chopped breaks on an Akai or MPC,
  Supernova, Virus, Moog-style basses.

## 2. Sound recipes (typical starting points, *unverified* unless cited)

- **Supersaw (JP-8000):** seven naive saws at 2x rate (88.2 kHz) through a highpass; "no modulation,
  no phase tricks, no chorus" ([Gearnews](https://www.gearnews.com/jp-8000-supersaw-synth/)). Ratios at
  maximum detune 0.8900, 0.9371, 0.9805, 1, 1.0199, 1.0622, 1.1075 (about ±11 %, slightly asymmetric)
  ([KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=8572590)). The detune knob is a steep polynomial,
  fine below about 50 % ([Scholl](https://schollz.com/til/221103)). Mix (Szabo): centre = -0.55366 m +
  0.99785; sides = -0.73764 m^2 + 1.2841 m + 0.044372. HPF tracks the fundamental; random phase at
  note-on. Patch: detune 30-60 %, mix 60-100 %, 4-8 voices, LP mostly open, release 200-500 ms, delay
  plus a 2.5-4 s hall.
- **Offbeat trance bass:** saw (± square), LP24 at 300-600 Hz, resonance 10-20 %, filter decay 80-150 ms,
  amp decay 120-200 ms, on 16th steps 3, 7, 11, 15, plus a sine sub. No sidechain needed.
- **Rolling prog bass:** 16ths with the kick step omitted (KBBB) or ducked; saw, decay 60-100 ms,
  offbeat accents, a slow cutoff LFO over 2-8 bars. The galloping KBBB is really late-90s/2000s psy;
  early goa used droning, offbeat or straight-16th basses ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa_trance)).
- **Pluck:** filter env attack 0, decay 150-300 ms, sustain 0, high env amount; 16ths into a 3/16
  ping-pong at 40-60 % feedback. Dotted 8th = 3/16.
- **Juno pad:** saw + PWM (LFO 0.3-1 Hz, about 35 %) + sub; LP 1.5-3 kHz; attack 0.3-1.5 s, release
  1-3 s; chorus I or II. The Juno chorus is an MN3009 BBD swept in about half its 0.64-12.8 ms range,
  L/R in antiphase ([ModularGrid](https://modulargrid.net/e/modules/view/43993)); rates about 0.5 Hz (I)
  and 0.8 Hz (II) *(unverified)*.
- **Gated pad:** originally a noise gate keyed from a hi-hat pattern
  ([Soundbridge](https://www.soundbridge.io/use-the-trance-gate-effect-to-alter-the-rhythm),
  [looperman](https://www.looperman.com/free-music-software/detail/mastercode-music-t-force-trance-gate-2));
  now a 16-step gate at 50-75 % duty with 2-10 ms edges.
- **Dub chord / stab:** m7, m9, m11, sus; short filter env into a 1/8 or 3/16 delay at 50-70 %
  feedback, HP about 300 Hz and LP about 4 kHz inside the loop, into a long reverb; cutoff ridden by
  hand (Bedrock, Way Out West). Attack's dub stab: 12 dB BP around 450 Hz + dotted 8th
  ([Attack](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/basic-channel-style-dub-techno/)).
  Chord memory transposes it in parallel.
- **Acid:** slide is constant-time, about 60 ms whatever the interval
  ([KVR](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=63251)); accent boosts filter sweep and volume
  ([nt_303](https://github.com/thorinside/nt_303)).
- **Goa extras:** Phrygian, Phrygian dominant, harmonic minor, double harmonic; mostly single lines over
  a root drone; squelchy resonant 16ths, S&H on cutoff, ring mod, sync, 2-op FM blips, phasers, panning,
  zaps (fast downward pitch env plus noise), self-oscillating sweeps, delay throws.
- **Prog breaks:** rolling "Moog" bass (mono, LP24 low, glide 20-60 ms, syncopated 16ths with octave
  jumps, drive); Reese (two saws ±5-15 cents, LP, drive); HP'd, chopped, bit-reduced breaks with a 909
  or 808 kick under them; pads and orchestral strings.
- **Risers and builds:** noise through BP or HP swept over 8-16 bars with a level ramp, or a reversed
  cymbal; snare roll 4ths → 8ths → 16ths → 32nds with rising level, pitch and HP; crash on the 1.
- **Kick:** a tuned 909 driven through the desk. "Rumble kick" is a 2010s techno technique.
- **Sidechain pump:** mostly a French-house idiom ([Ableton](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/sidechain-compression-part-1/));
  light in 90s trance, more in the revival.
- Organ / M1 piano: more early-90s house; low priority.

**Tempo and harmony**
- Prog house 125-132 (Bedrock's "For What You Dream Of" is 127); trance 132-140 ("Greece 2000" is 132);
  goa 135-145; revival 128-155; prog breaks 125-135.
- Essentially no swing, except light swing on hats (prog house percussion 52-56 %; programmed breaks
  hats 55-60 %).
- Minor keys; i-VI-VII, i-iv-VII-V (harmonic-minor V), the Dorian IV.

## 3. Effects

- **Delay:** 3/16 (= dotted 8th: 0.75 x 60000/BPM, 326 ms at 138) or 1/8, ping-pong, feedback 25-60 %
  (dub to about 70 %; goa throws to self-oscillation with time modulated for pitch warps); LP 3-5 kHz on
  the repeats; 1/8 triplet for goa.
- **Reverb:** halls 2.5-4 s on leads and pads (breakdowns 3-6 s), pre-delay 20-60 ms; plates and rooms
  (0.8-1.2 s) on snares and claps; HP the input at 200-300 Hz. Quadraverb types: plate, room, chamber,
  hall, reverse ([Muzines](https://www.muzines.co.uk/articles/alesis-quadraverb/1377)).
- **Modulation:** Juno chorus; 4- and 6-stage phasers shared across parts; flangers on hats and rides.
- **Drive:** desk drive on kicks and basses; distortion on acid; bit/sample-rate reduction on breaks.
- **Master:** moderate bus compression (2:1, 10-30 ms attack); heavy limiting is a modern habit.

## 4. Arrangement

- 12" mixes run 6-11 minutes ("Set in Stone" 11:04, "Waters" Phase III 8:10, "Gen Z" 7:26).
- Intro and outro: 16-32 bars of drums, mirrored.
- Something enters or leaves every 8 or 16 bars; sections land on 32-bar lines; a fill in the last bar
  of each phrase. Psytrance reworks leads and rhythms on an 8-bar cycle
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_trance)).
- The main breakdown comes at about 50-65 % of the track (16-32 bars, up to 64 in anthems), then an
  8-16 bar build (snare roll, filter or noise riser), then a crash on the 1. Filter sweeps run 16-64
  bars.
- Prog house: the slow build, the bass entering late, elements taken out rather than added. Goa: fewer
  euphoric breakdowns, more FX breakdowns and false endings.

## 5. Sequencing

- 16th arps (up, up/down, random, octave jumps), gate 30-70 %; chord memory; gated chords; 303 accents
  and slides.
- The bass avoids the kick: offbeat (trance), KBBB (goa and rolling trance), syncopated 16ths (prog and
  breaks). Bass usually stays on the root while the chords above change.
- Polymeter: 3- and 5-step cells over 4/4 (goa, prog, acid); the 3/16 delay gives the same feel. Per-track
  "reset each bar/phrase" vs "free-run" matters.
- Velocity accents on hats and plucks; straight timing.

## 6. The reference tracks

Tracks 1-4: reviewer descriptions plus era norms, nobody has listened yet. Track 5: decoded.

**Cass & Slide, "Perception"** (1999, Automatic Records; on Sasha's GU Ibiza and Tiësto's ISOS2;
[Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/master/34225-Cass-Slide-Perception))
- "Takes a long time before the bass starts to wander in… goose bump time"; "dark, epic"; "slow build
  up to the crescendo" ([Random Various](https://randomvarious.tumblr.com/post/622756933725831168/cass-vs-slide-perception-global-underground)).
  A vocal mix exists; the B-side is stripped and slower.
- Probably 132-136 BPM *(unverified)*; A minor appears only on a 2025 remix listing.
- Needs: a rolling bass entering late with cutoff opening over 32+ bars; long-release pad; plucked or saw
  melody into a 3/16 delay; noise and snare build; song lanes spanning many bars.

**Taucher, "Waters"** (1996; Phase III mix 8:10 on Dance Pool DAN 663836 2 and Liquid LQS 004-6; 1998
remixes; [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/master/100573-Taucher-Waters))
- Produced by Torsten Stenzel; has lyrics. "Deep floaty melodic trance", at the shift from hard trance to
  the about-140 BPM sound. Probably 136-140 *(unverified)*.
- Needs: 909 four-to-the-floor with offbeat open hats; offbeat or rolling bass; a sustained minor
  melody; big pads, reverb and delay; a long breakdown; optional vocal sample.

**Bedrock, "Set in Stone"** (1997, Stress 12STR 80, 11:04; Bedrock Records started in 1999;
[Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/release/39418-John-Digweed-and-Nick-Muir-Present-Bedrock-Set-In-Stone))
- "Twisting synth dub", "driving melodica", "epic and melodic". One upload says 122 BPM
  ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wIJWlghH_Ag)); the era norm is 125-130: both unconfirmed.
- Needs: dub stabs into feedback delay with the cutoff ridden by hand; a rolling 16th bass; a hypnotic
  sequenced line; a long DJ structure with intro and outro of 32+ bars.

**Oprofessionell, "Gen Z"** (2022, Ute.Rec UTE008 track 3, 7:26; mixed by Francis Latreille, mastered by
Nik Kozub; [Bandcamp](https://ute-rec.bandcamp.com/album/ute008))
- 153 BPM ([decks.de](https://www.decks.de/track/oprofessionell-ute008/cjh-pm)); tagged Trance and
  Ambient; "nostalgia, psychedelia and hyper-emotions". No gear information.
- Likely needs *(unverified)*: fast 16th bass with ducking, supersaw or pad chords, goa-style resonant
  riffs, washy reverb, ambient breakdowns.

**T.G.ViRUS, "TGV's KiloMix '98"** (the ReBirth 2.0 demo song; parsed from `public/songs/tgv-kilomix-98.rbs`
in [varyform/re-rebirth](https://github.com/varyform/re-rebirth) with `tools/rbs_dump.py` and
`tools/rbs_timeline.py`)
- A megamix of four songs ("Base Of Love", "Adrenaline", "Moonflight", "Secret Love!"). 143 BPM, a
  547-bar loop (about 15:18). Global shuffle 69/127, switched on only for the 909 hat patterns: swung hats
  over straight acid.
- **303 #1, the lead acid:** cutoff 0, resonance 123, env mod 0, decay 74, through distortion (amount and
  shape 127), delay send 117/127. Played live: about 6,000 cutoff moves and 4,300 resonance moves, up to
  about 250 cutoff moves per 8 bars; distortion amount and shape swept too (about 2,300 moves).
- **303 #2, the bass/pluck:** cutoff 127, resonance 0, env mod 127, decay 0. Bright and unresonant,
  shaped by the PCF rather than knobs (about 250 moves).
- **Interlock:** in song A, #1 plays legato 8ths high (slide plus tie) while #2 plays roots F, G/G#, C#,
  D# on steps 0, 2, 6, 10, 12, 14, syncopated against them. Patterns 8-15: both play 3-step cells (note,
  note-with-slide, tie: 3+3+3+3+4), a built-in 3/16 polyrhythm. Transposed one-bar patterns switched
  every bar make progressions out of mono lines: C-F-Bb-G (i-iv-VII-V with a B natural) and D-F-A-G (D
  Dorian). Role swaps around bar 384 (#1 plays 28-31 while #2 plays 24-27; at 408 both play 24-27).
  One-bar patterns alternate A/B or ABAC to make 2- and 4-bar phrases.
- **The PCF:** a tempo-synced filter envelope retriggered by a 32-slot pattern from 56 shapes, in effect a
  trance gate on the cutoff. On #2 it starts on wave 4 (a hit every 8th) at frequency 42, resonance 101,
  amount 44, decay 45; later ramp shapes, frequency swept between 37 and 93.
- **Effects:** delay 3 sixteenths, feedback 64 rising to about 74 (about 55 %), its pan swept constantly
  (about 600 moves). Delay sends: #1 117, #2 82, 808 103, 909 0 (kick dry). Compressor on the 808 channel
  only (amount 127, threshold 10).
- **Drums:** 909 kick tune 127, attack 0, decay 65. Fills: 16 accented snare 16ths in the bar before a
  change; a crash-only pattern at section starts; a crash on every beat at climaxes; accents on every
  step with a broken kick. The 808 plays percussion: a 3-3-2 cowbell, toms, rimshot, offbeat cymbals.
- **Structure:** 32 bars of drums only; changes at bars 32, 120/128, 208, 264, 328, 384, 456; breakdowns
  at 304-327 (no drums, solo acid with cutoff riding) and 368-383 (both 303s on the progression); an outro
  reprising song A.
- **A modern take adds:** sustained harmony (pads, supersaw chords), reverb (ReBirth had none), a sub
  under the 303s, ducking from the kick, stereo ping-pong, per-step locks beside the live riding, noise
  risers, longer patterns.

## 7. Ranked primitives (best coverage first)

1. Polyphonic VA, 4-8 voices: polyBLEP saw and pulse with PWM; supersaw mode (Szabo curves, pitch-tracking
   HPF); sub; noise.
2. LP24 ladder plus a multimode SVF (LP/BP/HP); the 303 filter.
3. Two exponential ADSRs with velocity and accent; two synced LFOs (tri, square, S&H); glide.
4. A tempo-synced 16/32-step modulator to amplitude (gate) or cutoff (PCF).
5. Synced stereo delay (1/8, 3/16, 1/4, 1/8T), ping-pong, feedback to 90 %, filters in the loop.
6. A long hall/plate, 2-6 s, pre-delay, damping.
7. Juno BBD chorus (I/II) and a 4/6-stage phaser.
8. Per-channel drive with a shape control; master glue compressor.
9. Arpeggiator and chord memory (m7, m9, sus; up/down/random; octaves; gate).
10. Song mode: per-bar pattern switching, 8-64-bar automation lanes, phrase fills, mutes on phrase lines.
11. Kick-triggered ducking; a noise riser.
12. Extras: 2-op FM or ring mod for goa blips; the break player for prog breaks.
